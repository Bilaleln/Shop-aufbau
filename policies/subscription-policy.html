How Subscribe & Save Works

  • It's a recurring purchase. When you choose Subscribe & Save, you are signing up for repeat orders, not a one-time purchase.
  • Price: each subscription order is 15% off the regular one-time price of the product. The price for your subscription, plus any applicable shipping and taxes, is shown at checkout before you buy.
  • Frequency: you choose a delivery frequency of every 30, 60, or 90 days. You are charged and a new order is created once per selected interval.
  • Automatic charges: by subscribing, you authorize us to charge your payment method automatically for each order at the frequency you selected until you cancel.
  • No minimum commitment and no cancellation fee.

How to Cancel

You can cancel at any time before your next order is processed and charged:

  • through the subscription management option in your customer account, where available; or
  • by emailing us at badush71@proton.me with the email address used for your order and the request "Cancel my subscription."

Cancellation stops future orders. An order that has already been processed or shipped before your cancellation can't be cancelled, but it remains covered by our Return & Refund Policy.
