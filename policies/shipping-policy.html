This Shipping Policy applies to orders placed on the FALUNARA online store at falurana.com, operated by M and B the MBFs LLC ("FALUNARA," "we," "us").

Where We Ship

We currently sell to customers with shipping addresses in the United States.

Order Processing

Orders are processed after payment has been confirmed. You'll receive an order confirmation email after you place your order and a shipping confirmation email when your order ships.

Shipping Charges

Available shipping methods and shipping charges are calculated and displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase.

Tracking

Where tracking is available for your shipment, the tracking information will be included in your shipping confirmation email.

Delays

Delivery times can be affected by factors outside our control, such as carrier delays, weather, or high shipping volumes. If we can't ship your order within the time we've indicated, or within 30 days of your order if no time was indicated, we'll notify you and give you the option to cancel your order for a full refund.

Incorrect or Incomplete Addresses

Please check your shipping address carefully at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us as soon as possible. We'll try to update the address if your order hasn't shipped yet, but we can't guarantee changes after an order has been processed. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we'll contact you to arrange next steps.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your tracking shows your package as delivered but you haven't received it, or your package hasn't arrived, please contact us. We'll look into it with the carrier. If your order arrives damaged, please see the "Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items" section of our Return & Refund Policy.

Questions
