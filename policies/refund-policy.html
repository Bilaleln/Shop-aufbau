30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be happy with your FALUNARA Botanical Body Oil. If you're not satisfied, you can request a return and refund within 30 days of the date your order was delivered. A product that has been opened and used as intended may still qualify under this guarantee.

This 30-day guarantee is our own store policy. It does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.

How to Request a Return

  1. Contact us at badush71@proton.me within 30 days of delivery. Please include your order number and a short description of the reason for your request.
  2. We'll review your request. Once it's approved, we'll send you return instructions, including the address the item should be returned to.
  3. Return the item following those instructions, together with any remaining product and the original packaging where reasonably possible.

Please don't send items back without contacting us first. Returns sent without an approved request, or sent to an address other than the one in our return instructions, may not be able to be processed.

Return Shipping Costs

For change-of-mind and money-back-guarantee returns, the customer is normally responsible for the cost of return shipping. Original shipping charges are normally non-refundable, except where required by law or where we are responsible for the issue (see below).

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, or you received the wrong item, please email us at badush71@proton.me as soon as possible, and within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and photos of the item and packaging. If we confirm the issue, we will make it right at no extra cost to you, for example with a replacement or a refund (including the related shipping charges), as appropriate. We may not need you to return the item in these cases. If we do, we'll cover the return shipping.

Refunds

Once your return has been received and processed, or your request has otherwise been approved, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. After we issue a refund, your bank or card issuer may need additional time to process it and show it on your statement.

Subscription Orders

Cancelling a subscription stops future orders; it does not by itself refund an order that has already been processed. If you'd like to return a subscription order, the steps above apply. See our Subscription Policy for how to cancel.

Exclusions and Fair Use

To keep this guarantee fair for everyone, we may decline a refund where:

  • the request is made more than 30 days after delivery;
  • the container is returned empty, or the product appears to have been intentionally used up before a return was requested;
  • the item was not purchased from the FALUNARA online store; or
  • we reasonably believe the request is fraudulent or abusive, including excessive or repeated return requests.

Nothing in this section limits any rights you may have under applicable law.
