We want you to love your FALUNARA Botanical Body Oil. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can request a full refund within 30 days of the date your order was delivered.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Email us at badush71@proton.me within 30 days of delivery.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, or you received the wrong item, please email us at badush71@proton.me as soon as possible, and no later than 30 days after delivery. Include your order number and a photo of the item and its packaging so we can review the issue and make it right.

Refunds

Once your return has been received and reviewed, we'll email you to confirm your refund. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take several business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

Late or Missing Refunds

If we've confirmed your refund and you haven't seen it yet, please check with your bank or card issuer first. If you still need help, contact us at badush71@proton.me.
