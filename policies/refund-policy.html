Refund & Return Policy

Falurana is operated by M and B the MBFs LLC (Florida, USA). We want you to love your purchase. If you are not satisfied, you may request a return within 30 days of receiving your order.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused, unopened, and in their original packaging with the seal intact. For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used personal-care products cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.
  • A receipt or proof of purchase (order number) is required.

How to start a return

Email us at badush71@proton.me with your order number. If your return is approved, we will send you instructions on how and where to send your package. Items sent back without first requesting a return will not be accepted.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

Please inspect your order on arrival and contact us within 7 days if an item is damaged, defective, or incorrect, so we can make it right at no cost to you.

Refunds

We will notify you once we have received and inspected your return. If approved, your refund will be issued to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may need additional time to post the refund. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different item is to return the original and place a new order.

Sale items

Items purchased on final sale or with a gift card cannot be refunded unless they arrive damaged or defective.
