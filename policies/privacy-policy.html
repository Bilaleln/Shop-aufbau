Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how M and B the MBFs LLC, a Florida limited liability company that operates Falurana ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from falurana.com (the "Store"). The Store is hosted by Shopify Inc.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, billing and shipping address, email address, phone number.
  • Order and payment information: items purchased, transaction details, and payment confirmation. Card data is processed by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Account information: if you create an account, your login details and preferences.
  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and similar data collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact customer support.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill orders, handle returns, and provide customer support.
  • To send order updates and, with your consent where required, marketing emails or text messages. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and to keep the Store secure.
  • To improve the Store and our products.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

How we share your information

We share personal information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as Shopify (hosting and checkout), payment processors, shipping carriers, and analytics or marketing partners, and when required by law. We do not sell your personal information for money. Some advertising cookies may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under certain US state laws; you can opt out as described below.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your state of residence (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, and others), you may have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; correct inaccurate information; delete your information; obtain a portable copy; and opt out of targeted advertising, "sale," or "sharing" of personal information. To exercise any of these rights, email us at badush71@proton.me. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by law. You may use an authorized agent, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as an opt-out where required.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to run the Store, remember your cart, and understand how visitors use the Store. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

The Store is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 for "sale"/"sharing" purposes).

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, including to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
