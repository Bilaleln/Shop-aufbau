Itching

The menopause experience

It's started slow, the insides of my arms at the elbow crease would itch and I would get hives, or they looked like hives, then two weeks ago it woke me up at 11:30 , my whole body, but it's not just itching, it's like prickly things as if I've got something poisonous flowing through my body. I have not had a period for about 4 years, but I have PMS symptoms EVERY month. I find I have gained weight. My skin is drier, except my face that is, but that's nothing new. I have my annual next month. About 5 years ago she tested me and said I was not meno. I am going to get her to check me again. I use lavender in a diffuser at night and take Benadryl b/c I cannot sleep any longer and my itching is relatively non-existent, but about mid-morning, it comes back, some days are worse than others. I have noticed I get the occasional hot flash of late, but not bad, I did have them bad about 6 years ago for about a month. I seem to be sweating more though b/c I notice the smell, sorry if TMI. Also my brain is missing some days, like I say the wrong thing and say, where did that come from? What the heck is going on? Menopause? I will be 55 in Dec. Thank y'all!!
