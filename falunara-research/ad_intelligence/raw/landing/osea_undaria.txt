FAQs
Is it normal to have sediment at the bottom of my Undaria Algae™ Body Oil?

Yes! This oil contains Undaria seaweed, so you may find naturally occurring seaweed sediment at the bottom of the bottle. It may not be visible at first, as it takes time to settle. This is normal and doesn't affect the product's quality or efficacy.

How do I layer Undaria Algae Body Oil with other products?

After cleansing with our Undaria Algae™ Body Wash, apply Hyaluronic Body Serum to dry or damp skin from the neck down. Then, generously massage Undaria Algae™ Body Oil as the last step in your body care ritual for silky, glowing skin. Undaria Algae™ Body Lotion or Undaria Algae™ Body Butter also work great layered under Undaria Algae™ Body Oil for an added boost of hydration.

Is it normal to see color or smell variance with my Undaria Algae™ Body Oil?

It is typical for the Undaria Algae™ Body Oil to range in color from golden to emerald green. The color does not impact the quality or efficacy of the product.

To create this one-of-a-kind oil, we soak hand-harvested Undaria algae in barrels of oils to infuse the vitamins and minerals. Due to the artisanal manner of production, variation in color is normal.

We use all-natural ingredients to create the fragrance of our products. The scent may vary slightly from batch to batch.

What does Undaria Algae™ Body Oil smell like?

Uplifting grapefruit, lime and cypress combined with the sweet notes of mango mandarin.

Can I use Undaria Algae™ Body Oil on my face?

Undaria Algae™ Body Oil has been formulated for use on the body, so we don't suggest it for the face. To experience the benefits of Undaria seaweed on your face, try our Dayglow Face Oil™ - they are created in a similar way, however, Dayglow Face Oil™ is a moisturizing face oil that delivers instant radiance to dry, sensitive skin.

How do I unlock and use the pump on my product?

Here's a step-by-step guide to help you unlock and use your pump effectively:

1. Hold your product firmly to prevent it from spinning as you try to unlock the pump.
2. Locate the small white plastic circle right below the pump's neck and hold it tightly.
3. Using your other hand, turn the pump counter clockwise to unlock it. Note that you do not need to push down while you twist, as our pumps do not 'pop up' once unlocked.
4. To prime the pump, press down a few times until the product dispenses.

What is the packaging made of and how can I recycle it?
Packaging:	Bottle is made of frosted glass and pump is made with 50% PCR (post-consumer recycled) plastic.
Recycling:	Remove pump and dispose of it in the trash. Clean bottle and put in your curbside recycling bin*.
*Before disposing in your recycling bin, please check if your local curbside recycling program accepts frosted glass.

If you live in the Los Angeles area, you can participate in our Recycling Program at our Venice Skincare Studio. Find all the details here.
